# Marswalk: sources and attribution (English)

This list is an English translation of `Sim/SOURCES.md` (14 sources), plus the NASA and other pages cited in the repository documents. Where the source states no license, it says **License: not stated in the source**. No license has been assumed. Only the USGS 1 m DTM states a license (CC0 1.0), and that license is stated in the source.

Translation note: `Sim/SOURCES.md` is generated by `scripts/build-sources-list.mjs`. The Turkish file is the record. Where the Turkish "Uygulamadaki kullanım" (use in the app) line conflicts with `Sim/DEMO_NOTES.md`, this list follows `DEMO_NOTES.md` and flags the conflict.

## The 14 sources

### 1. USGS Mars 2020 TRN HiRISE DTM mosaic (local 1 m elevation window)

- **Product ID:** `JEZ_hirise_soc_006_DTM_MOLAtopography_DeltaGeoid_1m_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_blend40`
- **Resolution:** 1 m/pixel. Source mosaic 21488 rows x 21400 columns. The app loads a 1676 x 1135 window.
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (stated in the source: "This work is marked with CC0 1.0 Universal")
- **Attribution:** USGS Astrogeology Science Center (Fergason, Hare, Mayer, Galuszka, Redding, Smith, Shinaman) in collab. with NASA JPL. Fergason, R. L., et al., 2020, 51st LPSC, Abstract #2020. DOI: 10.5066/P9REJ9JN
- **Address:** https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/mars2020_trn/HiRISE/JEZ_hirise_soc_006_DTM_MOLAtopography_DeltaGeoid_1m_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_blend40_pds3.lbl
- **License proof:** https://www.usgs.gov/data/mars-2020-terrain-relative-navigation-hirise-dtm-mosaic
- **Use in the app:** Analysis window for slope, route checks and the ortho alignment test (`Sim/data/dtm_subset.f32`). **Not displayed:** the app's data registry rejects it because its vertical datum is not proven, and falls back to the legacy window (source 2).
- **Limits:** The vertical datum is not verified for this product (`verticalDatumVerified: false` in `Sim/data/dtm_subset.json`). No absolute elevation claim is made. The CC0 statement does not extend to the ortho mosaic (source 3).

### 2. HiRISE DTEEC DTM (legacy local window, currently displayed)

- **Product ID:** `DTEEC_045994_1985_046060_1985_U01`. PDS dataset `MRO-M-HIRISE-5-DTM-V1.0`.
- **Resolution:** about 1.0096633268711 m/pixel
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **License proof:** not stated in the source (the PDS label has no license field)
- **Attribution:** ALFRED MCEWEN, USGS-ASTROGEOLOGY (as written in `Sim/SOURCES.md`)
- **Address:** not stated in the source
- **Use in the app:** Shown as the active terrain: ground elevation, slope, route cost and 3D terrain (`Sim/data/legacy-hirise/`). It is the app's fail-closed fallback.
- **Limits:** Which terrain to keep is an open product decision (K3-7 in the internal roadmap).

### 3. NASA JPL Mars Trek WMTS: MRO HiRISE Jezero controlled orthomosaic

- **Product ID:** `JEZ_hirise_soc_006_orthoMosaic_25cm_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_first_dd`
- **Resolution:** 25 cm nominal. Downloaded tiles: z17 = 0.318 m/px to z13 = 5.088 m/px. The nominal 25 cm is not met by the downloaded tiles.
- **License:** not stated in the source (the FGDC Use_Constraints and Access_Constraints fields are empty)
- **Attribution:** USGS/NASA MRO HiRISE Controlled Orthomosaic (25cm resolution) over Jezero crater TRN basemap; generated by the USGS Astrogeology Science Center (per FGDC abstract); served by NASA JPL Mars Trek
- **Address:** https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/TrekWS/rest/cat/metadata/fgdc/html?label=JEZ_hirise_soc_006_orthoMosaic_25cm_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_first_dd
- **Use in the app:** Image overlay (Hybrid and Ortho modes), from `data/mars-trek/ortho-tiles` and `data/ortho/*.png`
- **Limits:** Half-pixel alignment is not proven. Alignment is measured as +/-1.5 m on the USGS analysis window only. The DTM CC0 statement does not apply to this mosaic.

### 4. NASA JPL Mars Trek FGDC record: MRO HiRISE Jezero DEM (controlled)

- **Product ID:** `JEZ_hirise_soc_006_DTM_MOLAtopography_DeltaGeoid_1m_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_blend40` (same as source 1)
- **Resolution:** 1 m (manifest definition)
- **License:** not stated in the source (Use_Constraints and Access_Constraints fields are empty)
- **Attribution:** NASA JPL Mars Trek and Mars 2020 Perseverance Terrain / Imagery / Traverse Dataset
- **Address:** https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/TrekWS/rest/cat/metadata/fgdc/html?label=JEZ_hirise_soc_006_DTM_MOLAtopography_DeltaGeoid_1m_Eqc_latTs0_lon0_blend40
- **Use in the app:** Metadata and frame comparison only

### 5. NASA Mars 2020 MMGIS archive traverse and waypoints (M20_traverse.json, M20_waypoints.json)

- **Resolution:** Vector data (points and lines); the resolution concept does not apply
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **License proof:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA JPL Mars Trek and Mars 2020 Perseverance Terrain / Imagery / Traverse Dataset
- **Address:** https://mars.nasa.gov/mmgis-maps/M20/Layers/json/M20_traverse.json
- **Use in the app:** Archived rover track layer and waypoint check. The rover track is not a human walking route and is not PLACES data.
- **Limits:** Archive data, not live telemetry.

### 6. USGS SIM 3464: geologic map of Jezero crater and Nili Planum

- **Product:** USGS Scientific Investigations Map 3464
- **Resolution:** map scale 1:75,000 (vector)
- **License:** not stated in the source. The supplied FGDC metadata and service information contain no usage or license text (copyrightText is empty).
- **Attribution:** Sun, V.Z., and Stack, K.M., 2020, Geologic map of Jezero crater and the Nili Planum region, Mars: U.S. Geological Survey Scientific Investigations Map 3464, pamphlet 14 p., 1 sheet, scale 1:75,000, https://doi.org/10.3133/sim3464
- **Address:** https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3464/ and https://services.arcgis.com/v01gqwM5QqNysAAi/ArcGIS/rest/services/Jezero_FeatureServiceFromPro/FeatureServer
- **Use in the app:** Geologic unit boundaries and distance to contact (`data/usgs-sim3464-geology`)
- **Limits:** At 1:75,000 scale the boundaries are not as accurate as the 1 m DTM.

### 7. NASA/JPL-Caltech Mars 2020 Perseverance raw image archive (Sol 120 and Sol 164)

- **Product:** 12 raw images from Navcam, Hazcam, SuperCam, Mastcam-Z and CacheCam. The app uses JPEG previews (file suffix `_1200`).
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **License proof:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA/JPL-Caltech
- **Address:** https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020-raw-images/
- **Use in the app:** Photo gallery at the Sol 120 and Sol 164 stops (`data/perseverance-raw`)
- **Limits:** Raw, not calibrated for science. The viewing direction is an estimate.

### 8. NASA Mars Trek MOLA ImageServer regional elevation window

- **Product ID:** `mola128_mola64_merge_90Nto90S_SimpleC_clon0`
- **Resolution:** 463 m nominal; 384 x 384 cells; 0.0078125 degrees
- **License:** not stated in the source (copyrightText is empty)
- **Attribution:** NASA Mars Trek / MOLA (`mola128_mola64_merge_90Nto90S_SimpleC_clon0`)
- **Address:** https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/trekarcgis/rest/services/mola128_mola64_merge_90Nto90S_SimpleC_clon0/ImageServer
- **Use in the app:** Regional context and horizon (`data/regional-dem`). It does not produce a walkability score.
- **Limits:** Status `needs_review`. The vertical datum and latitude convention are not verified.

### 9. USGS Astrogeology Viking MDIM 2.1 color global mosaic (tile pyramid)

- **Product:** `Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_global_232m.tif`
- **Resolution:** 231.5417872 m/pixel native (pyramid levels z0 to z3)
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **License proof:** not stated in the source (no credit or license text in the file)
- **Attribution:** Viking MDIM 2.1 color global mosaic, from the file name. No credit line is recorded in the repository.
- **Address:** not stated in the source
- **Use in the app:** Global Mars context on the minimap (`data/viking-mdim`). Not an orthoimage and not elevation.
- **Limits:** A processed color product. Its colors are not a true measurement of terrain color, and 232 m/px cannot be used as local texture.

### 10. NASA Planet Compare: Mars surface gravity

- **Product:** Mars surface gravity, 3.71 m/s² (single rounded value)
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA
- **Address:** https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/planet-compare/?intent=121
- **Use in the app:** Vertical acceleration in the jump physics (`Sim/public/js/config.js`)
- **Limits:** A global reference, not a Jezero measurement. The NSSDC fact sheet gives 3.73 m/s² (rounding difference, not verified further).

### 11. NASA NSSDC Mars Fact Sheet

- **Address:** https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA NSSDC
- **Use in the app:** Gravity comparison (3.73 m/s²); mean surface pressure 6.36 mbar (seasonal range 4.0 to 8.7 mbar); density about 0.016 +/- 0.006 kg/m³; semi-major axis 1.52366231 AU
- **Limits:** Average and seasonal values, not a Jezero instantaneous measurement. No atmospheric drag is added to the movement physics.

### 12. NASA NSSDC Sun Fact Sheet

- **Address:** https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA NSSDC
- **Use in the app:** Apparent solar diameter at 1 AU, 1919 arcseconds, scaled to Mars distance in the sky model (`sky-model.js`)
- **Limits:** Only the disk size comes from this source. Sky colors are presentation parameters.

### 13. NASA Photojournal PIA19400 "Sunset in Mars' Gale Crater"

- **Address:** https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/sunset-in-mars-gale-crater
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA Photojournal. The credit line is not stored in the repository and should be copied from the page before use.
- **Use in the app:** Qualitative reference: fine dust scatters blue light around the sun at sunset (`sky-model.js`)
- **Limits:** Qualitative only. No color or intensity numbers come from this source.

### 14. NASA GISS Mars24 sunlight algorithm

- **Address:** https://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/mars24/help/algorithm.html
- **License:** not stated in the source
- **Attribution:** NASA GISS
- **Use in the app:** Sun direction and declination (`geo/mars-sun.js`)
- **Limits:** Scenario parameters: L_s 0 degrees, local true solar time 15:00. Not a live measurement.
